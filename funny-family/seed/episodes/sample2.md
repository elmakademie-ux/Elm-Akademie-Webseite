sample: true

EMMA (calm): The volcano must not be touched. Scientifically speaking.

BOB (sleepy): What is that smell? Is it lunch?

The volcano erupts foam all over Bob. Lucy appears in the doorway.
BOB (surprised): My sandwich has been... promoted!
LUCY (amused): Emma, do we need a second experiment?
SFX: big foam splat

## Scene 4 | Kitchen | 8s

ROSE (cackling): Needs more salt.
EMMA (deadpan): Grandma. That is the experiment.
