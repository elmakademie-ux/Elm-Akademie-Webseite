# Episode 1: The Missing Remote
season: 1
sample: true
logline: Bob hides the TV remote so he never has to get up again.

## Scene 1 | Living room | 7s
Bob is stretched across the sofa. The TV is on a boring weather channel.
BOB (yawning): The remote is exactly where I left it. Somewhere. Far away.
SFX: cushion squeak
MUSIC: lazy upbeat jazz bass

## Scene 2 | Living room | 8s
Lucy walks in holding laundry and stops, one eyebrow raised.
LUCY (dry): Bob, the remote is in your hand.
BOB (panicked): That is not a remote. That is a... small, rectangular TV friend.

## Scene 3 | Living room | 8s
Emma sits on the carpet writing in her tiny notebook.
EMMA (deadpan): Dad, you said the same thing yesterday. About the sandwich.
MAX (scheming): Dad hid the remote in the sofa. Again!
SFX: comedic boing

## Scene 4 | Living room | 9s
Grandma Rose enters on her cane and presses a button on a tiny remote. The TV switches to a wrestling show.
ROSE (sweetly): I changed the channel, dear. The weather was lying to us.
BOB (shocked): Mum, that is my TV friend!
SFX: crowd cheering
